Olive x James

Olive x James is a popular café and matcha spot located on Gardener St and Melrose Ave, Los Angeles known for its relaxing atmosphere, quality drinks, and modern style. The café is especially loved for their ceremonial matcha. It has become a favorite place for people to study, meet with friends, or simply enjoy a good drink. Its welcoming environment and beautifully made drinks have helped it stand out as one of the go-to café spots in Los Angeles.
