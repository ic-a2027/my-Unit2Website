Community Goods

Community Goods is located on Ediburgh and Melrose Ave. They are known for their elevated coffee program, signature matcha drinks, and stylish atmosphere that have made it a popular destination in the Melrose area. Community Goods has become a go-to spot for locals and visitors looking to enjoy quality coffee, casual dining, and the vibrant culture of Los Angeles.
