Rōk Matcha and Café

Rōk Matcha is a fun and modern café located on Ardmore and Olympic in West Hollywood, Los Angeles, that has become a favorite spot for matcha lovers and coffee fans alike. Known for its smooth ceremonial matcha, creative drinks, and cozy atmosphere, the café gives people a relaxing place to hang out, study, or catch up with friends.
